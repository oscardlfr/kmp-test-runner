## Why the campaign was revised

The first Evidence4 primary attempt stopped after a worker timeout. Its 1,860-second watchdog left only 60 seconds above the provider's 1,800-second timeout, while accepted sessions already showed 92–133 seconds of setup before provider start and 16–21 seconds after provider end. That timing proved an inadequate containment margin; the failed cell's underlying provider state was not observed. The original attempt, its accepted cells and its costs remain private and excluded. A subsequent prospective transport fail-fast fix and a new Evidence4-specific four-cell canary preceded this fully new 32-position design. Neither pilot nor canary contributes to the reported n.

## Frozen analysis and stop rules

The cross-arm primary metric is exact key-fact agreement on the structured answer, with every scheduled and counted denominator disclosed. Product protocol success is a separate product-arm metric. Accepted semantic negatives stay in the sample; rejected/structurally absent cells remain missing without score imputation. The sensitivity analysis excludes only cells flagged by the frozen infrastructure classifier. A block replacement would require a frozen qualifying failure class, exact safe closure and custody, a fresh campaign ID and no answer-based selection. None was used for this revised primary campaign.

Stop public performance claims for any transcript access hit, unexplained context-relevant agent-state change, four or more rejected/missing/provider-runtime-error cells, six or more suspected infrastructure flakes, fewer than six counted cells in a runtime/arm, failed identity/custody/merge/control checks or an exceeded USD 50 primary-attempt ceiling. Missing accepted-cell usage is a cost-control failure, never zero. A lost post-dispatch transport cell without usage would incur the predeclared USD 25 administrative reserve and a budget recheck; that case did not occur in this revised campaign.

Every block required an exact-VM Off/VHD-detached preflight, source and manifest checks, readiness/dry-run receipts and mutually exclusive live execution. Before the next block, its predecessor needed `Closed PASS`, network isolation, independent raw and public/private hash custody, access/infra/cost review and independent controls audit. The published [controls audit](controls-audit.md) records what those checks showed and what remains unverified.
