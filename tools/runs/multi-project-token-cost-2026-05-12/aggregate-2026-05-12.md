### Per-bucket spread observations

- **Small** spread is large (837%) — driven by kmp-production-sample's near-zero test output (2 modules, very few tests). KaMPKit and kotlinconf-app both land in the 60–120× range.
- **Medium** spread is tight (7%) — both PeopleInSpace and Confetti come in within a few % of each other despite very different module counts (7 vs 13). The 89–96× range is a solid medium-bucket estimate.
- **Large** spread is enormous (5,521%) — NowInAndroid's measurement (top-level walker, 5 of 35 modules sampled) understates the A baseline; `private-large-A` used an explicit module filter to sample representatively. Treat the bucket median as an artifact of methodology, not project scale. Re-measuring NowInAndroid with `--module-filter "**"` is queued for v0.10 step 7.
